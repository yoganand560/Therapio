About Therapio

Bridging the Gap in Mental Wellness

Therapio is a comprehensive mental wellness platform designed to address the growing mental health challenges faced by individuals in today's fast-paced world. We connect people facing stress, anxiety, and depression with certified psychologists while offering immediate support through our free AI mental health assistant powered by Google Gemini.

Our mission is to make mental health support accessible, affordable, and effective for everyone. We believe that emotional well-being is a fundamental human right, and technology can play a transformative role in delivering mental health services to those who need them most.

With a focus on students and employees who face unique pressures in academic and professional environments, Therapio provides tailored support to help manage stress, improve emotional resilience, and enhance overall quality of life.
